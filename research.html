MRI derived hippocampal asymmetry identifies hippocampal sclerosis in epilepsy surgical specimens

Ndagijimana P, Brennan D, Shinohara RT, Gugger JJ. Brain Communications. 2026;8(4):fcag320.

Describes the AutoHS hippocampal asymmetry workflow for research and surgical cohort validation. Implementations: AutoHS (pipeline) and NeuroInsight-AutoHS (application).
